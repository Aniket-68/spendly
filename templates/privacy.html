Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles your information

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve the service: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to generate the summaries you see in the app. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may delete your expenses or your account at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
